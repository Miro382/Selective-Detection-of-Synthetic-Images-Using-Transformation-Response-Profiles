Research code, fitted selector parameters, anonymized numerical evaluation
outputs, and scripts for reproducing the reported analyses are publicly
available at:

https://github.com/Miro382/Selective-Detection-of-Synthetic-Images-Using-Transformation-Response-Profiles

The repository includes experiment configurations and source fingerprints.
Dataset images, third-party annotations, and detector checkpoint binaries are
not redistributed; the corresponding providers and asset requirements are
documented in the repository. The supplied numerical checks reproduce the
supported analyses from stored outputs without detector inference. Recreating
the full training history requires the separately obtained model and data
assets identified in the repository.
