DATA SOURCES AND ACCESS

This repository distributes numerical experimental outputs, source code,
configuration, and provenance fingerprints. It does not distribute dataset
images, thumbnails, source-image filenames, prompts, identity annotations,
downloaded dataset archives, or pretrained detector weights.

Stored scores support analysis of the recorded predictions. They do not replace
the original images for training, detector inference, or a new content-overlap
audit. Opaque sample and group identifiers preserve pairing and resampling
structure; they are not an image-retrieval manifest. A source hash identifies
an archived artifact and does not establish public availability of that artifact.

MAIN COHORTS

DeepShield, version 1.0, Truebees Srl (2025)
Provider: https://zenodo.org/records/15648378
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15648378
The provider describes the dataset as CC BY 4.0. The experiments used PreSocial
images. DeepShield contributed to detector adaptation and selector fitting,
so its principal evaluation cohort is an in-domain held-out test.
The independent ablation uses newly selected images from the same source.

OpenFake, Complex Data Lab
Provider: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ComplexDataLab/OpenFake
Archived experiment revision: 3247b942f18d39d1fd476b1077852af033d35d8c
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09495
The current provider card labels the dataset CC BY-NC 4.0. The experimental
revision is recorded separately because the dataset can change. OpenFake is
the principal cross-dataset test relative to the documented fitting sources.
Later reference and adapted-detector analyses reuse the original test cohort;
they are not additional independently sampled replications. The independent
ablation uses its separately audited 6,000-image OpenFake cohort.

ADDITIONAL TRAINING AND HISTORICAL EVALUATION SOURCES

CelebA, Chinese University of Hong Kong
Provider: https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/projects/CelebA.html
The provider limits access to non-commercial research and prohibits further
publication or distribution of dataset portions. Original In-The-Wild images
and identity annotations must be obtained from the provider. The detector
training configuration uses identity-aware selection; those images and
annotations are not included here.

Community Forensics evaluation set, Owens Lab
Provider: https://huggingface.co/datasets/OwensLab/CommunityForensics-Eval
Archived experiment revision: 7d4a74a88d2cac93b513c0853bf92c260eaceea0
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04125
The current provider card labels the evaluation set CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and
describes non-commercial research and educational use. Its real-image sources
have their own provenance. It appears in the historical evaluations and
fixed-checkpoint stress tests, rather than the principal OpenFake comparison.

Synthbuster, Quentin Bammey
Provider: https://zenodo.org/records/10066460
Paper: https://doi.org/10.1109/OJSP.2023.3337714
The provider distributes synthetic images from nine generators and describes
their relationship to RAISE-1k source images. Obtain the images and their
applicable terms directly from the provider. No Synthbuster images or prompts
are included in this repository.

RAISE, University of Trento
Provider: https://loki.disi.unitn.it/RAISE/
The provider supplies the real images used with Synthbuster in historical
evaluations and requests citation of the RAISE study. Obtain the dataset
separately; no RAW files, JPEG derivatives, or source metadata are bundled.

Earlier local detector training
The local development archive includes an AI-vs-human-generated collection
obtained through Kaggle and earlier checkpoints whose complete training-source
provenance is not recoverable from this release. Historical aggregates are
retained with that limitation. The repository does not claim complete source
independence for those earlier checkpoints or distribute that collection.

ACCESS AND REDISTRIBUTION

Provider pages were checked on 3 November 2026. The license labels above report
what those pages state; they do not grant rights to underlying third-party
photographs or to later dataset versions. Consult the provider's current terms
before obtaining or using any dataset. Numerical verification does not download
images. A complete training rerun requires
separately obtained data and the corresponding experiment inputs.
